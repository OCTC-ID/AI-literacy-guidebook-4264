AI Label Maker
Mark the AI level at the top of each Blackboard assignment.
Workbook  ·  Tool
Make a label

Choose a level, adjust the wording if you like, and copy the label into the top of the assignment instructions in Blackboard. Using the same label on every assignment helps students find the level quickly.

Preview

Use Copy label to paste straight into the Blackboard editor. If the formatting doesn't hold, use Copy HTML and paste it into the editor's HTML option. The dots and wording come through either way.

Which Level?

No AI

Students complete the work on their own. Use it when the skill itself is what the assignment teaches.

AI-supported

Students can use AI for the parts you name. The thinking and the final product are theirs. Replace the brackets in the label with those parts.

AI-integrated

Learning to use AI well is part of the assignment. Point students to the steps in the instructions that tell them how.
